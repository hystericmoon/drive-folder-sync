Drive Folder Sync

A personal Android app that keeps folders on your phone and folders in your Google Drive in sync, in both directions.

What it does

  • Pairs a phone folder you pick with a Drive folder you pick, including subfolders.
  • Syncs every 15 minutes in the background, or on demand. Wi-Fi-only and charging-only options are set per pair.
  • Never deletes permanently. Files removed on one side go to Drive's trash or to a .sync-trash folder on the phone.
  • Keeps both copies when a file changes on both sides at once.

Who it's for

This is a personal project, built and used by its developer. It isn't distributed through Google Play. The source code is public on GitHub.
